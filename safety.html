Live Progress

see what is happening during the ride.

For solo rides, this gives riders a personal record they can trust. For group rides, it gives organizers better awareness of who is riding, where the group stands, and how the day is progressing.

  • Track daily solo rides without extra setup overhead
  • Track group participation with a clearer live overview
  • Keep ride timing and rider status easy to understand
  • Make the ride feel active, not static

Emergency Setup

emergency contacts are part of rider trust.

Throttle does not keep safety abstract. Riders can save up to 3 emergency contacts, which makes the app feel more prepared before a ride even begins.

  • Store up to 3 emergency contacts inside the app
  • Keep names, relation, and phone details easy to manage
  • Reinforce that safety is designed in, not added later

Pre-Ride Information

less confusion before the road begins.

Throttle keeps the ride briefing visible: route, meeting point, visibility level, rider limit, notes, and setup. That matters because a smoother ride usually starts before the ride starts.

  • Meeting point and destination in one place
  • Visibility settings for public or private ride control
  • Notes and rules shared before riders arrive
  • Better coordination when clubs or groups get larger

real sos screens.

These screens make the promise concrete: captain broadcast, checkpoint control, and one-tap SOS actions for situations that need fast communication.

Captain broadcast and SOS entry

Ride leaders can broadcast context and keep the group aligned while the SOS bar remains visible at the bottom of the screen.

Actionable SOS options
