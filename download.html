Installation Guide

HOW TO INSTALL THE APP.

Installing direct Android (APK) or iOS (IPA) packages takes less than 60 seconds. Follow these simple steps:

01

Download The File

Click the Download Android APK button above. Your browser will prompt you to confirm downloading the throttle-latest.apk package.

Tip: If your browser shows a safety warning, tap "Download anyway". The APK is built directly from our repository.
02

Allow Unknown Apps

Once downloaded, tap the notification or open the file from your Downloads folder. If prompted by Android, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

Tip: This is a standard Android permission required for any application installed outside of Google Play.
03

Install & Hit The Road

Tap Install on the prompt. Once finished, open Throttle, sign in or register your rider account, add your bikes, and ignite your first ride!

Tip: You can update future versions without uninstalling or losing your saved ride logs and statistics.
